FocusTube

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

FocusTube is a browser extension designed to help users reduce distracting surfaces on YouTube.

Data Collection

FocusTube does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Local Settings

The extension stores user preferences locally in the browser, such as whether Shorts, the home feed, related videos, or comments should be hidden, and whether direct Shorts links should be redirected or allowed.

Website Content

FocusTube processes YouTube page content locally in the browser only to identify and hide selected distracting surfaces. No page content, browsing data, or user activity is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Remote Code

All extension logic runs locally in the browser. FocusTube does not use remote hosted code.
